# PFLX Knowledge Digest (X-Bot brain)

This file is served at `/public/pflx-knowledge.md` and auto-loaded into X-Bot's
knowledge base on boot and every 15 minutes. Edit it to teach X-Bot new facts —
no redeploy of logic required, just update this file. Keep it in plain Markdown.

## What PFLX is
PrototypeFLX (PFLX) is a gamified, project-based learning platform. Players
(students) progress through a Season of Checkpoints, Projects, and Tasks, earning
X-Coin (XC) and Digital Badges. Hosts (teachers) run everything from Mission
Control. Sub-apps: Core Pathways (learning modules), Battle Arena (competitions),
Open Space (exploration/combat economy), DarkCampus (project comms), X-Coin (the
economy + badges + modifiers).

## Getting started (players)
1. Sign in with your brand name + PIN.
2. Open X-Bot (the floating icon) for help any time — it knows the platform.
3. Check Mission Control / your task list for what's assigned to you.
4. Submit tasks for host approval; approved tasks award XC and badges.
5. Track your rank in Evolution Rankings; climb by earning total XC.

## Ranks (unlock by total XC)
1 Player 1,000 · 2 Advanced Player 5,000 · 3 Apprentice 15,000 · 4 Manager/Head
50,000 · 5 Director/Producer 125,000 · 6 Mentor/Intern 250,000 · 7 Associate
400,000 · 8 Senior 600,000 · 9 Chief 800,000 · 10 Partner 1,000,000. Higher ranks
also require completed checkpoints and specific badges.

## Badges
Earned from tasks, checkpoints, and host awards. Categories: Primary (behavior),
Premium (achievement), Executive (jobs), Signature (skill mastery / certs). Each
badge carries an XC value and artwork; earning one credits XC and shows in your
portfolio.

## Modifiers (Upgrades, Fines, Penalties)
Set up in X-Coin, they function across the whole platform. Upgrades/bonuses add
XC, multiply XC for a time, or extend deadlines. Fines/penalties deduct XC or
freeze activity for missed deadlines, incomplete submissions, or off-task
behavior. Some fire automatically on events; hosts can also apply any manually.

## Chat & X-Bot
The floating X-Bot icon opens a dock with two tabs: X-BOT (AI assistant) and P2P
CHAT (direct + group messages, with video/screen calls). Click an online player
in the Live roster to DM them. The icon glows green when you have unread messages.
X-Bot can read messages aloud.

## Host FAQ
- Approving a task awards its XC + badges to the submitter and marks it complete.
- The Progress dashboard shows each player's progress, scoped to your tier.
- Host broadcasts post a real-time "NEW BROADCAST — open X-Bot" ticker alert and
  land in X-Bot + the Mission Control General chat.

## FeedForward
FeedForward is the beta-tester feedback form. To let X-Bot learn from responses,
publish the responses sheet to the web as CSV and add its URL under X-Bot →
Settings → Knowledge Base → Live Sources. X-Bot will fold it in and re-read it
automatically.
