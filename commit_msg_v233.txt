PATCH PLATFORM v233: Story Mode — the season played as a campaign

Reporter: Ennis. Ask: "build story mode. X-Live will integrate with this as
well", with the BRANDBUILDER / THE ALTER EGO Activity Guide attached.

Root cause of what this replaces: the two flagship projects lived only as a
Google Doc checklist and a pile of Canva and Gemini links. A player had no
single surface telling them where they were in the season, no in-platform
place to do the work, and nothing awarded X-Coin for any of it — so the
currency and the curriculum were running on separate rails.

Story Mode is that surface. A seventh top-level view, a Home App Hub tile,
and a 28-quest campaign across seven acts:

  Act Zero   Arrival — Tessera's briefing, studio select
  Act One    The Alter Ego — Personality Trait Inventory, Character Forge,
             Character Profile, Brand Board, and the minted Alter Ego card
  Act Two    BrandBuilder Phase One — client select, an RPG client interview,
             Client Profile, a drag-and-drop Empathy Map, Checkpoint 3
  Act Three  BrandBuilder Phase Two — the three SparkLab rounds, the combined
             concept, Checkpoint 4
  Act Four   The Developer's Workshop — starting state and a repeatable
             Daily Growth Log, wired to ProtoDev AI
  Act Five   FeedForward Force — a five-question mini course, then give and
             take feedforward
  Act Six    Exhibit — booth design, the ninety-second pitch, the Showcase
             on X-Live, and self evaluation

The eight clients are the leads of The Nexus Narratives, and every interview
answer is drawn from that character's graphic-audio script, so the story a
student listens to is literally the brief they then solve. Eight questions
per client, two each revealing Says, Thinks, Does and Feels, so a completed
interview fills every quadrant of the Empathy Map.

Discipline notes:
- Every quest reward goes through PflxDataBus.award(pid, {xc, source:'story'}).
  Nothing mutates .xc directly, so modifiers, rank-up and the ticker all still
  fire on one code path.
- Progress is per player in app_data key pflx_story_<playerId>, mirrored to
  localStorage, using pflxCloudKvPush / pflxCloudKvLoad like everything else.
- Quest completion broadcasts pflx_story_progress to every sub-app iframe.
- The Showcase quest calls navigateTo('lite'), which is the X-Live hook.
- Module is sentinel-wrapped (PATCH PLATFORM v233) for slicing and reverting.
- Art: 40 plates extracted from the Nexus Narratives PDF into public/story-art/.

Tests: test_story_v233.js — 31 assertions, including a real vm run of the
campaign graph (unique ids, no forward prerequisites, every quest pays) and
of the client data (8 clients x 8 questions x four quadrants). Syntax gate
clean at 28 blocks. The same module passed 32 browser assertions in a
harness before it was inlined.
